Before you buy

What to expect.

Does this change my lock screen too?
No. That's an Apple-only surface — this replaces your desktop, not your lock screen.
Do I need an Apple Music subscription?
Yes. It reads straight from Music.app — no Spotify, no free-tier account.
Will the lyrics always line up exactly?
Close, not frame-perfect — timings come from LRCLIB, a community database. No synced lyrics for a track? The words space out evenly instead.
The menu bar icon disappeared — is it broken?
Probably not — a full menu bar can hide new icons. Press ⌥⇧W anywhere to toggle it instead.
I force‑quit it, and it just came back. Why?
By design — it stays running the way your desktop picture does. Use the menu bar toggle to actually turn it off.
Apple Silicon or Intel — does it matter?
Either is fine — it's a universal binary, native on both.
